Experiment Controls

Step 1: Select "No Masker (Baseline)" and find your absolute threshold for various probe frequencies. Save each.
Step 2: Select a Masker (e.g., 1000 Hz).
Step 3: Play the tone. Adjust the Probe Level until you can *just* hear it over the masker.
Step 4: Click "Save Threshold". Repeat for probes below, near, and above the masker.
Suggested testing around 1000Hz: 500, 700, 850, 950, 1000, 1050, 1200, 1500, 2000
